# Daughter Kingdom / 女儿国

Faithful vernacular retelling of the Daughter Kingdom episode from Jinghua Yuan (chapters 32–37) by Li Ruzhen, Qing dynasty.

## Notes

- Original text: public-domain ancient Chinese text. This adaptation is by Muse, with plot, characters and ending kept as in the original, no added characters, no changed ending. Note the two separate returns to the ship in the original: the official release after the river works, and the later night rescue of Prince Yin Ruohua.
- Images: AI-generated illustrations, not photos. Tang Ao wears a blue-grey scholar robe; Lin Zhiyang, clean-shaven after his beard was burned off, wears a rose-purple robe and green skirt as consort; Duo Jiugong is white-bearded; the Daughter King wears a red-gold royal robe.
