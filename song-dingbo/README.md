# Song Dingbo Catches a Ghost / 宋定伯捉鬼

Faithful vernacular retelling of "Song Dingbo Catches a Ghost" from Soushenji by Gan Bao.

## Contents in this folder

- `zh/song-dingbo.md` — Chinese full version
- `images/` — 3 illustrations, both characters in every image

## Image positions

1. `images/1-night-meeting.webp` — after the night meeting paragraph, before the turn-taking carrying paragraph
2. `images/2-river-carry.webp` — after the river crossing paragraph, in the carrying and splashing scene
3. `images/3-market-sheep.webp` — after the market paragraph, Song Dingbo selling the sheep the ghost turned into

## Notes

- Original text: public-domain ancient Chinese text. This adaptation is by Muse, with plot, characters and ending kept as in the original, no added characters, no changed ending.
- Images: AI-generated illustrations, not photos. Characters: Song Dingbo only, and the ghost, who later turns into a sheep.
- Publishing: Toutiao account "浠水文艺", to be updated after submission.
