# Nie Xiaoqian / 聂小倩

Ancient Chinese text adaptation project: a faithful vernacular retelling of "Nie Xiaoqian" from Liaozhai Zhiyi by Pu Songling, in Chinese and English, with AI-generated illustrations in the correct story positions.

## Contents

- `article/zh/nie-xiaoqian.md` — Chinese full version, title: 聂小倩：兰若寺的人鬼相救
- `article/en/nie-xiaoqian.md` — English full version, plus the X thread draft at the end
- `images/` — 4 illustrations, same faces across images

## Image positions

1. `images/1-north-courtyard.webp` — after the north courtyard night talk, before the first bedroom visit
2. `images/2-temple-night.webp` — after the first bedroom visit and gold ingot refusal, in the temple night scene with Yan Chixia and his sword bag
3. `images/3-ning-home.webp` — after the evening routine at Ning's home, in the home scene with Ning's mother and his sick wife
4. `images/4-sword-bag.webp` — after the final night-fork demon scene with the sword bag, before the jinshi ending

## Notes

- Original text: public-domain ancient Chinese text. This adaptation is by Muse, with plot, characters and ending kept as in the original, no added characters, no changed ending.
- Images: AI-generated illustrations, not photos. Nie Xiaoqian wears pale blue-white robes and holds a blue lantern. Ning Caichen wears a blue-grey scholar robe. Yan Chixia carries a worn leather sword bag.
